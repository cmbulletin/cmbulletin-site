Lighthouse Gold issues 35 million shares to option a Guyana gold property

The CSE has accepted the property option deal for filing, though transfer of Guyana's mining permits still needs approval from the country's mines regulator.

Lighthouse Gold Inc. (CSE: LGHT) has received Canadian Securities Exchange acceptance for its acquisition of the Mariwa–Sardine Hill–Lower Cuyuni gold property in Guyana's Oko Gold District, under a property option purchase agreement with 1464911 B.C. Ltd. It has paid US$150,000 cash and issued 35,000,000 shares at C$0.185 each, worth C$6,475,000, plus a US$100,000 deposit paid earlier.

The 35,000,000 shares equal about 15.98% of Lighthouse Gold's outstanding shares and carry a resale restriction of at least four months and a day. The company holds the right to acquire a 100% interest, but the permits transfer only once Guyana's mines regulator, the GGMC, approves it.

CSE · 5 Nov

Copper One's 19.9 million special warrants convert into shares and warrants

The automatic conversion, completing a September financing, hands holders new common shares plus warrants exercisable at $0.50 for two years.

Copper One Resources will see 19,889,750 special warrants, issued September 29, 2026, automatically convert into common shares and warrants on November 6, 2026, the company said. The special warrants comprised 12,383,000 non-flow-through and 7,506,750 flow-through warrants; holders need take no action.

The conversion delivers 19,889,750 common shares and 9,944,875 share purchase warrants. Each warrant lets the holder buy one common share at $0.50 for two years from the offering's closing date; the company may accelerate that expiry on 30 days' notice if the shares trade at $1.25 or higher for five straight sessions on the CSE.

CopperCorp plans to raise up to C$3 million and shrink its share count

The explorer's non-brokered placement pairs units and subscription receipts with a one-for-five share consolidation ahead of TSX Venture Exchange approval.

CopperCorp Resources, which is exploring for copper, gold and rare earth elements at its AMC project in western Tasmania, Australia, plans a non-brokered private placement of up to C$3.0 million. The raise combines up to 4,444,444 units at $0.1125 each, for up to $500,000, and up to 18,518,519 subscription receipts at $0.135 each, for up to $2.5 million.

Each unit carries one post-consolidation share and one warrant exercisable at $0.18 for 24 months; each subscription receipt converts, once escrow conditions clear, into a unit of one share and half a warrant exercisable at $0.24 for 24 months. The conversion depends on completion of the company's Skyline Project sale. Shares carry the usual four-month-and-a-day hold.

TSXV · 4 Nov

Bastion Square Partners II raises $1.5 million in its TSX Venture debut

The Vancouver capital pool company sold 15 million shares at 10 cents each and will look for an acquisition target to complete its qualifying transaction.

Bastion Square Partners II Inc., a Vancouver-based capital pool company, closed its initial public offering on November 3, 2026, raising $1.5 million in gross proceeds through the sale of 15,000,000 common shares at $0.10 each. The shares listed on the TSX Venture Exchange that day under a trading halt pending closing.

Trading in the shares, under the symbol BASQ.P, is set to resume November 9, 2026. The company now has 27,000,000 shares outstanding, of which 13,825,000 are held in escrow. Independent Trading Group (ITG), Inc. acted as agent, earning a $55,000 cash commission, a $10,000 corporate finance fee and 500,000 warrants exercisable at $0.10 until November 3, 2028.
