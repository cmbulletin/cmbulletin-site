OpenAI cuts its funding target by $10 billion as AI stocks slide

The ChatGPT maker now seeks $20 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation, down from a $30 billion target.

Capital Markets Bulletin · October 31, 2026 · Source: Reuters · TSXV:PINK, CSE:SPAI, CSE:RAIL

OpenAI has cut the target for its latest funding round by $10 billion, now seeking about $20 billion rather than the $30 billion it had been pursuing, according to a Financial Times report carried by Reuters. The company is also seeking a lower valuation, $1.25 trillion rather than the $1.4 trillion target. OpenAI had set a Friday deadline for investor commitments, Reuters reported.

The reduction follows a selloff across technology stocks tied to concerns over the scale of AI infrastructure spending. Reuters reported the Nasdaq fell 2.9% on the day the cut was reported and is down 7.8% from its October 6 peak. Some investors have reduced the size of their commitments to the round, and the fund's 'first close' — the initial tranche of capital committed — is expected to come in below the revised $20 billion target.

Reuters named several participants in the round, including the UAE-backed technology fund MGX, expected to be the largest investor, alongside Founders Fund, Coatue, Sequoia Capital and Andreessen Horowitz. OpenAI is pursuing a public listing in 2027, and Reuters reported the company had been keen to close this round ahead of rival Anthropic's anticipated listing, since the two companies draw on an overlapping investor base.
