Goliath Resources raises C$9.8 million without issuing a single warrant

The non-brokered flow-through placement, still subject to final TSX Venture Exchange approval, funds exploration at the Golddigger-Surebet gold project in BC's Golden Triangle.

Capital Markets Bulletin · October 30, 2026 · Source: Investing News Network — Resource Investing · TSXV:GOT, OTCQB:GOTRF

Goliath Resources Limited, an explorer of precious-metals projects in the Golden Triangle of northwestern British Columbia, has closed a non-brokered private placement for gross proceeds of C$9,800,000. The company issued 6,057,000 flow-through common shares at a price of C$1.62 per share. No warrants were attached to the units, and the company paid no finder's fees or commissions in connection with the offering.

The shares were issued as flow-through shares, which qualify under subsection 66(15) of Canada's Income Tax Act. That designation lets the company renounce the associated tax deductions for qualifying exploration expenditures to the subscribers who bought the shares, rather than claiming those deductions itself — a structure used to attract investors to exploration-stage mining financings.

Goliath said the funds will go toward Canadian exploration expenses and flow-through mining expenditures on its Golddigger-Surebet Gold Project. The qualifying expenditures must be incurred by December 31, 2027, and the associated tax deductions renounced to subscribers by December 31, 2026.

The offering remains subject to final acceptance by the TSX Venture Exchange, the release states. The shares issued are subject to a statutory four-month hold period expiring March 1, 2027, under applicable Canadian securities law.

Chief executive Roger Rosmus said the financing is "an insurance policy that further enhances our strong balance sheet and puts us in a stronger position, with ~C$27.5M in cash going into 2027 and our planned exploration drill program."

The company describes its projects as sitting in "high quality geological settings and geopolitical safe jurisdictions amenable to mining in Canada." In 2026 it completed a fully funded 48,443-metre drill program on the Golddigger-Surebet project, for which assays are pending on 55 holes, following a 64,364-metre program in 2025.
