Permian Basin Royalty Trust sets date for unitholder vote

Record date is October 13 and the special meeting is set for November 23, 2026, to vote on a proposed business combination and incentive plan.

Capital Markets Bulletin · October 25, 2026 · Source: PR Newswire — financial services · NYSE:PBT

Argent Trust Company, as trustee ('Trustee') of Permian Basin Royalty Trust (NYSE: PBT), announced on October 23, 2026 that it has set a record date of October 13, 2026 and a special meeting date of November 23, 2026 for unitholders to vote on proposals tied to a previously disclosed Business Combination.

The special meeting was called at the request of SoftVest Advisors, LLC, together with other holders of trust units who, the release states, collectively own more than 15% of outstanding units.

The first proposal asks unitholders to approve and adopt the terms of an amendment to the Trust Indenture to effect the Business Combination pursuant to a Combination Agreement.

The second proposal asks unitholders to approve and adopt the terms of the PBT Land and Minerals, Inc. Omnibus Incentive Plan. The release does not describe what the plan covers beyond its name.

The third proposal would authorize an adjournment of the special meeting, if necessary or appropriate, to permit the solicitation of additional proxies.

The trustee states that it is not making any recommendation to unitholders as to how to vote on the proposals. It said additional details regarding the special meeting would be set forth in the trustee's notice, to be mailed to unitholders beginning October 23, 2026.

For the substance of the Business Combination and the incentive plan, the release directs unitholders to the trust's Form S-4 and Form S-1 registration statements filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission, rather than describing their terms itself.
