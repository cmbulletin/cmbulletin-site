Georgia Street Capital raises $250,000 in TSX Venture IPO

The capital pool company listed on the TSX Venture Exchange October 6 and begins searching for a business to acquire via a Qualifying Transaction.

Capital Markets Bulletin · October 7, 2026 · Source: TheNewswire · TSXV:GOST.P

Georgia Street Capital Corp. completed its initial public offering on October 6, 2026, issuing 2,500,000 common shares at $0.10 per share for gross proceeds of $250,000, and listed on the TSX Venture Exchange the same day as a capital pool company under the symbol GOST.P. The shares were listed and immediately halted pending completion of the offering; trading is expected to commence at the market open on or about October 8, 2026.

A capital pool company is a shell corporation listed under TSX Venture Exchange Policy 2.4 with no commercial operations and no assets other than cash raised in its initial offering. Georgia Street's release states the corporation intends to identify and evaluate businesses with a view to completing a Qualifying Transaction — the process by which a CPC acquires an operating business and converts from a shell into a regular listed issuer. No target or sector has been disclosed.

Leede Financial Inc. acted as agent for the offering. Its compensation was a cash commission equal to 10% of gross proceeds, a corporate finance fee of $15,000, and non-transferable options to purchase up to 250,000 shares at $0.10 per share, exercisable until October 6, 2031. The release discloses no other fees paid in connection with the offering.

Georgia Street now has 8,832,000 shares issued and outstanding. Of those, 6,632,000 shares held in aggregate by the corporation's directors, officers and seed shareholders are subject to escrow restrictions. The release does not state the length of the escrow or the conditions for its release.

The corporation's officers and directors are Steven Vanry, who holds the roles of chief executive, chief financial officer and corporate secretary in addition to his board seat, and directors Kevin Bottomley and Craig Roberts. The release discloses no further detail on their backgrounds or on how the seed shares were allocated among them.

The release does not state what Georgia Street intends to do with the $250,000 raised beyond its general purpose of pursuing a Qualifying Transaction, nor does it name any industry or target company under consideration. A reader should not infer that an acquisition is pending or imminent — none is disclosed.

The capital pool company mechanism is how the TSX Venture Exchange brings new shell vehicles to market ahead of an eventual business combination, and this listing follows that structure. Whether Georgia Street attracts a Qualifying Transaction, and on what terms, is not yet determined; the release discloses no timeline and no prospective target.
