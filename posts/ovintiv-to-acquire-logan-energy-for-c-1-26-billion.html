Ovintiv to acquire Logan Energy for C$1.26 billion

The all-cash deal adds about 266,000 net acres and 20,000 barrels of oil equivalent a day in the Montney's oil window, with closing expected early in 2027.

Capital Markets Bulletin · October 26, 2026 · Source: Investing News Network — Resource Investing · NYSE:OVV, TSX:OVV, TSXV:LGN

Ovintiv Inc. (NYSE:OVV, TSX:OVV) said it has agreed to acquire Logan Energy Corp. (TSXV:LGN) in an all-cash transaction. Logan shareholders will receive C$1.40 per share, for total consideration of approximately $890 million, or C$1.26 billion, including the assumption of Logan's net debt. The agreement was announced October 26, 2026, and the boards of both companies have unanimously approved it.

The transaction brings Ovintiv roughly 266,000 net acres in the oil-rich window of the Montney formation, along with about 20,000 barrels of oil equivalent a day of current production, the release said. Ovintiv said the acreage carries approximately 420 net oil well locations drilled on a 10,000-foot equivalent lateral basis, including about 220 it classifies as premium return locations, at an average cost of $1.5 million per location, and roughly 200 as upside locations.

The release said Logan's assets also include about 60,000 net acres with exposure to the Duvernay shale, two new oil batteries at Simonette with combined capacity of 24,000 barrels of oil a day, and interests in two natural gas processing facilities. These assets are included within the total consideration of approximately $890 million, or C$1.26 billion, that Ovintiv has agreed to pay, including the assumption of Logan's net debt.

Ovintiv said the acquisition is expected to be accretive to its 2027 Non-GAAP Cash Flow per Share by approximately 5% and to its Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow per Share by approximately 3%. The company also said it expects annual cost synergies of about $60 million and capital savings of more than $2 million per well, and that its net debt is expected to be below $4 billion following the transaction's close.

Ovintiv President and CEO Brendan McCracken said: "This bolt-on transaction delivers value for our shareholders by adding high margin oil and condensate production and infrastructure, utilizing our operational advantage to drive significant synergies, and adding to our already deep drilling inventory and future growth optionality." The release's own headline described the transaction as unlocking 'incremental profitability,' providing 'additional oil growth optionality' and adding to 'premium return drilling inventory.'

Completion is expected early in the first quarter of 2027, subject to Logan shareholder approval, court approval, customary regulatory approvals and other customary closing conditions, the release said. RBC Capital Markets is acting as exclusive financial advisor to Ovintiv on the transaction, with Blake, Cassels & Graydon LLP and Paul, Weiss, Rifkind, Wharton & Garrison LLP serving as Ovintiv's legal advisors. Ovintiv said its targeted shareholder returns are unchanged, with at least 60% of 2026 Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow expected to be returned to shareholders.
