PyroGenesis buys its Montreal plant for $3.1 million after a court fight

The plasma-technology company settled a Quebec court dispute over its purchase option before closing on the facility it has occupied since 2012.

Capital Markets Bulletin · November 4, 2026 · Source: Investing News Network — Resource Investing · TSX:PYR, OTCQX:PYRGF, FRA:8PY1

PyroGenesis Inc. (TSX: PYR, OTCQX: PYRGF, FRA: 8PY1), a plasma-based technology provider to heavy industry and defence, has completed its purchase of the Turcot manufacturing facility at 5655 Philippe-Turcot in Montreal for $3.1 million.

The building measures 40,902 square feet (3,800 square metres) and sits about 6.2 kilometres, or roughly a 10-minute drive, from the company's downtown Montreal headquarters. PyroGenesis has occupied the property since September 1, 2012.

The purchase settles an action PyroGenesis had filed in the Superior Court of Quebec, in which it sought to enforce a contractual option to buy the property that the building's owner had contested.

The closing followed completion of a comprehensive environmental assessment of the building and its surrounding property, and the closing of financing, the company said.

PyroGenesis said it plans to remain in the facility and continue operating there under a lease agreement, while it pursues a sale-leaseback of the property — an arrangement in which an owner sells a building and immediately leases it back from the buyer, converting the asset into cash while keeping the building in use.

The company's chief executive said PyroGenesis "acquired the property at a discount to its current market value" and that proceeds from a sale-leaseback would "provide additional capital to accelerate projects." PyroGenesis said it is in discussions with interested parties regarding such a transaction.

The building houses PyroGenesis's manufacturing activities, research and development programs, pilot plant systems, metal powder production, testing apparatus and office space, the company said.
