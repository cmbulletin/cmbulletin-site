Myriad Uranium closes its $1 million purchase of an Argentine project

The CSE-listed explorer paid cash and shares for 100% of the Los Altares uranium project in the Cerro Solo district.

Capital Markets Bulletin · November 2, 2026 · Source: Investing News Network — Resource Investing · CSE:M, TSXV:GCOM

Myriad Uranium Corp. (CSE: M; OTCQB: MYRUF; FSE: C3Q) said Monday it has closed its previously announced acquisition of the Los Altares uranium project in central Chubut Province, Argentina, from Green Shift Commodities Ltd. (TSXV: GCOM). The deal was first announced October 13, 2026, and the underlying share purchase agreement is dated October 9, 2026.

Myriad paid $300,000 in cash plus 1,571,268 of its common shares, valued at $0.4455 per share, or about $700,000, bringing total consideration to $1,000,000 for a 100% interest in Buenavida Metales S.A., the Argentine company that holds the project's mineral claims.

Green Shift retains a 2% net smelter return royalty on the project, with Myriad holding an option to repurchase half of that royalty, 1%, for $1,000,000. The shares issued to Green Shift as part of the consideration are subject to a four-month hold period, expiring March 1, 2027.

Los Altares covers 55,000 hectares in the Cerro Solo uranium district and is adjacent to the Cerro Solo deposit and the historical Los Adobes mine, according to the release, which also described the project as carrying significant historical drilling data and approved environmental impact assessments.

Separately, Myriad said it issued 150,000 stock options to a consultant, exercisable at $0.39 per share, vesting immediately and expiring October 30, 2031.

Myriad chief executive Thomas Lamb said the deal "continues Myriad's strategy of acquiring projects with significant historic work in known uranium districts and with low up-front and ongoing costs and strong leverage to the uranium price."
