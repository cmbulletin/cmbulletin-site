Saudi Aramco's profit jumps 34% to $36.25 billion despite attacks on its facilities

Elevated crude and product prices drove the profit rise, and the board authorized a $21.9 billion dividend, even as Houthi strikes hit its facilities and Iranian activity disrupted Hormuz shipping.

Capital Markets Bulletin · November 4, 2026 · Source: The National

Saudi Arabian Oil Co. reported third-quarter net profit of $36.25 billion, up 34% from a year earlier, as revenue climbed to $140.5 billion from $111.6 billion over the same period. The company attributed the increase to higher prices for crude oil and refined chemical products, even as sales volumes fell.

The results followed attacks on Aramco's operations: Houthi militants from Yemen launched missile and drone strikes targeting company facilities, including its Dhahran headquarters and refining operations in Jazan and Riyadh, while Iranian activity in the Strait of Hormuz disrupted shipping. Chief executive Amin Nasser said: "Geopolitical uncertainty and attacks on key energy facilities continued to amplify the global energy supply crisis in the third quarter of 2026."

Aramco's board authorized a base dividend of $21.9 billion for the third quarter, up 3.5% from a year earlier and payable in the fourth quarter of 2026. Capital expenditure for the quarter totaled $10.5 billion, down from $12.6 billion a year earlier, and the company said it is pursuing additional overseas storage capacity and alternative export routes to help maintain supply reliability amid the disruptions.
