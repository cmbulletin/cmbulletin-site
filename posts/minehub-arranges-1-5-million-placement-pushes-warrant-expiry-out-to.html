MineHub arranges $1.5 million placement, pushes warrant expiry out to 2027

The non-brokered financing prices units at $0.40 with $0.55 warrants, while more than 15.7 million existing warrants get an eleven-month extension.

Capital Markets Bulletin · October 27, 2026 · Source: Newsfile Corp (via finanznachrichten.de) · TSXV:MHUB, OTCQB:MHUBF

MineHub Technologies Inc. (TSXV: MHUB) (OTCQB: MHUBF) has arranged a non-brokered private placement to raise up to CAD $1,500,000. The offering consists of up to 3,750,000 units priced at $0.40 each. Each unit is made up of one common share and one-half of one transferable common share purchase warrant, with each whole warrant exercisable at $0.55 per common share for two years from the closing date.

A unit, in this structure, bundles a share together with the right to buy additional shares later at a fixed price — the warrant — giving subscribers upside if the stock trades above $0.55 before the warrant expires, without requiring them to pay for that right up front beyond the unit price.

Alongside the new placement, MineHub is extending the expiry of warrants it issued earlier. Prior warrants covering 14,584,954 shares at an exercise price of $0.40, issued January 31, 2024, along with 625,000 warrants at $0.50 issued July 25, 2025 and 550,463 warrants at $0.50 issued August 20, 2025, all had their expiry pushed from January 31, 2027 to December 31, 2027.

The company said certain insiders of MineHub may participate in the new placement, relying on exemptions from the formal valuation and minority shareholder approval requirements that would otherwise apply to insider participation under Multilateral Instrument 61-101.

Closing of the offering is subject to the company's receipt of TSX Venture Exchange approval, MineHub said. No closing date was given in the release.

MineHub said the proceeds are earmarked for working capital and for continuing to evaluate strategic, corporate and financial opportunities. The release gave no further detail on what those opportunities might involve.
