MetroCity Bankshares reports $24.3 million third-quarter net income

Diluted earnings per share rose to $0.84 for the quarter, with nine-month net income reaching $68.7 million, both ahead of the prior year.

October 25, 2026PR Newswire — financial servicesNASDAQ:MCBS

MetroCity Bankshares, Inc. (NASDAQ: MCBS), the Atlanta-based holding company for Metro City Bank, reported net income of $24.3 million, or $0.84 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2026 ended September 30, 2026, according to its earnings release dated October 23, 2026.

The third-quarter result compares with net income of $22.1 million, or $0.76 per diluted share, in the second quarter of 2026, and with $17.3 million, or $0.67 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025 — increases of 9.8% and 40.7%, respectively, as stated in the release.

For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the company reported net income of $68.7 million, or $2.37 per diluted share, compared with $50.4 million, or $1.96 per diluted share, for the nine months ended September 30, 2025, an increase of 36.4%.

For the third quarter, MetroCity reported return on average assets of 2.12%, return on average equity of 16.97% and a net interest margin of 4.07%, according to the release.

At September 30, 2026, the company reported total assets of $4.60 billion, gross loans held for investment of $3.98 billion and total deposits of $3.56 billion. Noninterest-bearing deposits were $828.3 million, representing 23.3% of total deposits, and uninsured deposits represented 34.7% of total deposits.

Dividends paid per share for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 totaled $0.87, compared with $0.71 for the same period in 2025. The release names Farid Tan as President and Interim Chief Financial Officer; no other executive is quoted.

The release includes cautionary language stating that actual results could differ from any forward-looking statements due to factors including economic and market conditions, changes in interest rates, competitive pressures in the banking industry, and regulatory developments. It provides no additional guidance beyond the figures reported for the quarter and nine-month period.
