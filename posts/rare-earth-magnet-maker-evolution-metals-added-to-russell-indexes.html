Rare-earth magnet maker Evolution Metals added to Russell indexes

The Nasdaq-listed critical-materials company joined the Russell 3000 and Russell 2000 at the September 21 open through FTSE Russell's quarterly additions.

September 21, 2026GlobeNewswire — Public CompaniesNASDAQ:EMAT

Evolution Metals & Technologies Corp. said its shares joined both the Russell 3000 and Russell 2000 indexes effective at the opening of US equity markets on September 21, 2026. The addition came through FTSE Russell's third-quarter 2026 quarterly process for recent initial public offerings, which folds newly listed companies into the indexes between the main annual reconstitution.

The Russell 3000 tracks the roughly 3,000 largest US-listed companies, and the Russell 2000 is its small-cap subset. FTSE Russell states that approximately US$12.2 trillion is benchmarked to or invested in its US index products, so index membership carries a mechanical consequence: funds that replicate the benchmarks must hold the new constituent in proportion to its weight, regardless of any view on the business.

That inflow is passive and formulaic rather than a judgement on value, and it is the substance of the event. Index inclusion does not raise capital for the company, change its share count or alter its operations; what it changes is the ownership base, pulling in index-tracking institutions and, often, widening research and trading coverage.

The company describes itself as a US-based critical materials and advanced manufacturing business producing rare-earth permanent magnets, battery materials and related critical minerals — a sector that has drawn policy attention as Western governments seek supply chains for magnets used in electric motors and defence hardware outside dominant incumbent producers.

Alongside the index news, Evolution Metals restated a set of operating targets: initial fiscal 2027 revenue guidance of US$400 million to US$460 million, a target of roughly 10,000 metric tons of annual production capacity, 6,000 metric tons of high-performance sintered NdFeB magnet capacity, a 750-megawatt power agreement with KEPCO, and thirteen additional ULVAC sintered-magnet production machines scheduled for delivery in November 2026.

Those figures are the company's own guidance and forward targets, not audited results, and a reader should treat them as management projections rather than reported performance. The release does not provide historical revenue or production against which to test them. What is externally verifiable is the fact of the index addition itself, which follows a published FTSE Russell methodology. Evolution Metals trades on Nasdaq as EMAT.
