SK Hynix is handing shareholders $75 billion after a sevenfold profit jump

The chipmaker's operating profit rose more than sevenfold to 83.3 trillion won as AI-driven demand for memory chips sold out capacity into 2029.

Capital Markets Bulletin · October 28, 2026 · Source: Reuters (via Lufkin Daily News)

SK Hynix said on Tuesday it will return approximately 100 trillion won, or about $75 billion, to shareholders this year, after third-quarter operating profit surged more than sevenfold to 83.3 trillion won from 11.4 trillion won a year earlier. Quarterly revenue rose 328% to 104.6 trillion won. The company is the world's largest maker of high-bandwidth memory chips used in artificial-intelligence data centres.

The shareholder return is split between roughly 30 trillion won in cash dividends and 60 trillion won in share repurchases and cancellations, the latter figure including the 40 trillion won in buybacks the company had already announced in August. Shares in SK Hynix rose as much as 9.4% following the announcement.

Chief financial officer Kim Woo-hyun said the company expects AI investment momentum to continue, with customers' AI spending translating into broader adoption of AI services and tangible business results. Analyst Ryu Young-ho of NH Investment & Securities said the results had eased many of the market's concerns, with earnings coming in ahead of expectations.

SK Hynix said it has sold out its high-bandwidth memory and DRAM chip production through 2029 and has signed long-term supply agreements with 14 major customers covering more than half of its planned 2027-2028 production capacity. The company said it expects memory-chip demand to continue exceeding supply through 2030.
