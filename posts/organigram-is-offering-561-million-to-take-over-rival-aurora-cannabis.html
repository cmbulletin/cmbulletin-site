Organigram is offering $561 million to take over rival Aurora Cannabis

The bid, worth $7.80 a share, tops Curaleaf Holdings' hostile offer and needs two-thirds shareholder approval at a December vote.

Capital Markets Bulletin · November 4, 2026 · Source: Globe and Mail — Business · TSX:ACB, TSXV:DB

Organigram Holdings has offered $7.80 a share for Aurora Cannabis, a deal the companies value at about $561 million. Aurora shareholders will be able to elect cash, 5.86 Organigram shares, or a blend of $3.75 in cash and 3.04 Organigram shares for each Aurora share they hold, giving them some choice over how much Organigram equity they end up owning.

The offer represents roughly a 21-per-cent premium to Curaleaf Holdings Inc.'s most recent bid of US$5 a share. Curaleaf, a Connecticut-based cannabis operator, has pursued a hostile takeover of Aurora for several months.

Completion requires at least two-thirds of the votes cast by Aurora shareholders in favour, at a special meeting the companies expect to hold in December.

Organigram has lined up financing to support the transaction: a $188-million private placement with British American Tobacco and $210 million in loan facilities from ATB Financial.

The companies said the combined business would operate five EU-GMP certified production and distribution facilities if the deal closes.
