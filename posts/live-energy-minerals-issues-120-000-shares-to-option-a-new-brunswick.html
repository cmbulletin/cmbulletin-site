Live Energy Minerals issues 120,000 shares to option a New Brunswick property

The CSE-listed junior's first tranche under an October 15 option agreement covers two uranium-copper claims near Plaster Rock, with no cash payments required.

Capital Markets Bulletin · November 1, 2026 · Source: Live Energy Minerals Corp. (via StockTitan) · CSE:LIVE, OTC:GTREF

Live Energy Minerals Corp. (CSE:LIVE; OTC:GTREF) said it completed the issuance of 120,000 common shares on October 30, 2026, at a deemed price of $0.20 per share, representing a deemed value of $24,000. The shares were issued as the initial tranche payment under an option agreement, dated October 15, 2026, granting Live Energy the right to acquire a 100% interest in the Plaster Rock property in New Brunswick.

The Plaster Rock property comprises two mineral claims totalling 73 units, which the release describes as a uranium-copper property. Under the option agreement, Live Energy must issue a further 1,000,000 common shares on or before October 30, 2027, and a further 2,880,000 common shares on or before October 30, 2028.

Each of the later tranches will be priced at the closing price of Live Energy's shares on the last trading day before issuance, under the policies of the Canadian Securities Exchange, the company said. The option carries no cash payments and no minimum exploration-expenditure commitments.

The optionor retains a 2% net smelter returns royalty on the property. Live Energy may purchase one-half of that royalty, equal to 1 percentage point, at any time for $500,000, the release said.

The 120,000 shares issued under the first tranche are subject to a statutory hold period expiring March 1, 2027, pursuant to applicable Canadian securities laws, the company said. The release does not designate its dollar figures as Canadian or US currency.

Live Energy Minerals also holds the Mesa Top Mine and Section 20 uranium claims in New Mexico and has optioned its McDermitt Lithium East project in Nevada to US Critical Metals Corp., according to the release.
