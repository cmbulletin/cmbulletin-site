Standard Uranium boosts its flow-through placement to $2 million

Strong demand led the Saskatchewan-focused explorer to increase the non-brokered offering to 25 million units at $0.08, with warrants exercisable at $0.12.

Capital Markets Bulletin · November 3, 2026 · Source: Investing News Network — Resource Investing · TSXV:STND, OTCQB:STTDF

Standard Uranium Ltd. (TSXV: STND, OTCQB: STTDF) has increased the size of a previously announced private placement, citing strong investor demand. The non-brokered offering, conducted under the listed issuer financing exemption in National Instrument 45-106, will now raise up to $2 million through the sale of as many as 25 million flow-through units priced at $0.08 each.

Each flow-through unit consists of one common share, issued under the flow-through provisions of the Income Tax Act, and one half of a common share purchase warrant. A whole warrant entitles its holder to buy one additional common share at $0.12, with the right to exercise beginning 61 days after closing and running until 24 months after that date.

A flow-through share lets the issuer renounce the associated tax deductions to the subscriber rather than claiming them itself, which is why flow-through financings are priced at a premium and used specifically to fund Canadian exploration spending. Standard Uranium said the net proceeds from this offering will go toward exploring its Saskatchewan uranium projects.

The release states the securities issued will not be subject to a resale hold period under applicable Canadian securities laws. Completion remains subject to acceptance by the TSX Venture Exchange, and finder's fees are payable to eligible parties who introduce subscribers to the offering, though no rate or specific finder is named.

The release does not give a closing date for the offering, and it does not state the size of the previously announced placement that this increase replaces, so the scale of today's upsizing cannot be measured against the original offer. Standard Uranium has not named a specific lead or sole finder in the release.
