Golden Shield signs binding LOI for C$6-million recapitalization

Walsh Karra Holdings would take an approximately 51% stake in the Guyana-focused gold company through subscription receipts priced at C$0.30.

Capital Markets Bulletin · October 13, 2026 · Source: Investing News Network — Resource Investing · CSE:GSRI

Golden Shield Resources Inc. (CSE: GSRI) said it has signed a binding letter of intent with Walsh Karra Holdings for a strategic investment and recapitalization of up to C$6,000,000. Walsh Karra, through Aurevo Resources Inc., would invest C$5,000,000 directly, with up to a further C$1,000,000 available to existing shareholders and management, with both tranches priced at C$0.30 per subscription receipt.

Each subscription receipt is structured to convert into one common share and one common share purchase warrant, with each warrant exercisable at C$0.39 for 24 months.

Proceeds are to be held in escrow pending a list of conditions: renewal and issuance of Prospecting Licence PL 1/2021 at Golden Shield's 100%-owned, 5,457-hectare Marudi Mountain Project in Guyana's Rupununi Mining District, along with board approval, shareholder approval, and acceptance by the Canadian Securities Exchange. If those conditions are not satisfied, the release said escrowed funds will be returned with interest.

On closing, Walsh Karra would hold approximately 51% of Golden Shield on a fully diluted basis, which the release identified as a change of control. Leo Hathaway is stepping down from his combined Chairman and CEO role, with a new CEO to be appointed at closing; Hathaway and a person identified as Kalyan will be retained as Special Advisors for 12 months, and the board will be reconstituted to include investor nominees and an independent director.

Marudi Mountain's Mazoa Hill deposit carries a 2017 historical estimate of 4.43 million tonnes grading 1.82 g/t gold (259,100 ounces) Indicated and 1.65 million tonnes grading 1.62 g/t gold (86,200 ounces) Inferred. The release stated that a qualified person has not done sufficient work to classify this historical estimate as current mineral resources or reserves, and that Golden Shield is not treating it as such.

The company separately said it will acquire, in exchange for 13,000,000 common shares, a permanent worldwide licence to use, deploy, commercialize, sublicense and embed what it called the SimplyFI AI, traceable minerals and blockchain technology stack in connection with Marudi Mountain and its broader Guyana platform. The release gave no further description of the technology or its intended function beyond this licence grant.

Golden Shield said there is a 90-day exclusivity period to negotiate a definitive agreement, and that the transaction is expected to close on or before January 6, 2027. The company said it may pay finder's fees of up to 5% of gross proceeds to eligible finders, and that all securities issued are subject to statutory hold periods and escrow requirements. The release did not name any agent or finder.

Gregory R. Walsh, Walsh Karra's co-founder, was quoted describing Guyana as 'one of the most compelling mining jurisdictions in the hemisphere,' calling Marudi 'a standout asset.' Walsh Karra described itself in the release as a U.S.-based investment firm with a portfolio spanning artificial intelligence, data-centre infrastructure, climate ventures, and mining and natural resources.

To verify independently

  • Walsh Karra Holdings' description of itself as holding a portfolio spanning AI, data-centre infrastructure, climate ventures, mining and natural resources is the company's own characterization.
  • The 2017 historical resource estimate at Mazoa Hill is sourced to the company and is explicitly flagged by the release as not compliant with current resource/reserve classification standards.
  • The characterization of Marudi Mountain as 'a standout asset' is a quoted opinion from Walsh Karra's co-founder, not an independent assessment.
