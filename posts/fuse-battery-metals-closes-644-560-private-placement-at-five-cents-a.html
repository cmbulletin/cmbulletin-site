Fuse Battery Metals closes $644,560 private placement at five cents a unit

The non-brokered deal priced units at five cents each, with insiders taking part and the financing still subject to TSX Venture Exchange approval.

Capital Markets Bulletin · October 30, 2026 · Source: TheNewswire.ca · TSXV:FUSE, OTC:FSBMF, FRA:43W3

Fuse Battery Metals Inc. (TSXV: FUSE, OTC: FSBMF, FRA: 43W3) is a Canadian exploration company focused on metals used in battery manufacturing, including cobalt properties near Cobalt, Ontario. The company said on October 29 that it has closed a non-brokered private placement of 12,891,200 units at $0.05 per unit, for gross proceeds of $644,560.

Each unit consists of one common share and one common share purchase warrant, with each warrant exercisable at $0.05 per share for five years from closing. Insiders of the company subscribed for 272,040 of the units in the placement, representing gross proceeds of $13,602.

In connection with the financing, Fuse issued 30,000 common shares at a deemed value of $0.05 per share as a finder's fee. All securities issued under the private placement, including the finder's shares, are subject to a statutory hold period expiring March 2, 2027 under applicable Canadian securities laws.

The private placement remains subject to final acceptance by the TSX Venture Exchange. Fuse said it intends to use the proceeds to pursue strategic acquisition opportunities, evaluate potential transactions, and support its ongoing corporate operations, working capital requirements and future business initiatives.

The release noted that more than CAD$25 million has been spent to date — stated in 2020 dollars, inflation-adjusted — on the company's Teledyne Property, one of the cobalt holdings the financing is intended to support alongside the company's other corporate purposes.

Beyond the financing terms and the brief reference to the Teledyne Property, the release gave no further operational update — no drill results, property description or revised budget — so this entry is limited to what the release discloses.
