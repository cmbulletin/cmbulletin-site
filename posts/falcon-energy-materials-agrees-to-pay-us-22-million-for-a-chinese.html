Falcon Energy Materials agrees to pay US$22 million for a Chinese graphite stake

The deal gives the TSX Venture-listed company a 49% stake in a profitable anode-material producer in Shandong province, funded by a private placement closed in September.

Capital Markets Bulletin · November 3, 2026 · Source: TheNewswire.ca · TSXV:FLCN, OTCQB:FLCNF

Falcon Energy Materials plc (TSX-V: FLCN; OTCQB: FLCNF) has agreed to acquire a 49% strategic, non-operating equity interest in Weihai Hensen New Materials Technology Co., Ltd., a Chinese producer of natural flake graphite and synthetic graphite anode materials, for US$22 million in cash. The transaction is being carried out through Falcon EMG Holdings Ltd, a wholly owned Falcon subsidiary, under an Equity Transfer Agreement with Weihai's sole equity holder.

Weihai is based in Shandong province and operates a production facility with capacity for 25,000 tonnes a year of coated spheronized purified graphite, or CSPG, a processed anode material. Founded in 2023, the company began commercial production in the second half of 2025. Falcon's announcement describes Weihai as profitable, selling its output under long-term contracts to leading Chinese anode material manufacturers.

Falcon describes the acquisition as a step in its strategy to become, in its own words, 'an integrated, global supplier of critical battery materials.' The 49% stake is structured as non-operating, so Falcon will not run Weihai's daily operations, but a shareholder agreement gives Falcon the right to nominate a director, a requirement that key decisions receive unanimous board approval, and rights of first offer and first refusal over Weihai's remaining equity.

Falcon said the purchase is fully funded from a C$34-million non-brokered private placement that it closed on September 23, 2026. A non-brokered placement is one sold directly to investors without an underwriting syndicate acting as agent.

The acquisition itself is expected to close by the end of the fourth quarter of 2026, subject to Chinese regulatory approvals, including registrations with the State Administration for Market Regulation and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange, as well as other customary closing conditions.

Falcon chief executive Matthieu Bos said the acquisition is 'a defining milestone for Falcon,' calling it the execution of commitments the company had previously made to its shareholders regarding its battery-materials strategy. Falcon's shares trade on the TSX Venture Exchange under the symbol FLCN and on the OTCQB marketplace under FLCNF.
