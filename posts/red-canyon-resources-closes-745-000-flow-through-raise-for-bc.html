Red Canyon Resources closes $745,000 flow-through raise for BC critical minerals

The non-brokered placement issued 3,725,000 flow-through units at $0.20 each, with half-warrants exercisable at $0.30 for 18 months.

October 27, 2026TheNewswire.caCSE:REDC, OTCQB:REDRF

Red Canyon Resources Ltd. (CSE: REDC) (OTCQB: REDRF) (Frankfurt: I91) has closed a non-brokered flow-through private placement for gross proceeds of $745,000. The offering consisted of 3,725,000 flow-through units priced at $0.20 each. Each FT Unit comprised one flow-through common share and one-half of one common share purchase warrant, with each whole warrant exercisable into one common share at $0.30 for an 18-month term.

A flow-through share lets the company renounce the associated Canadian exploration expenses to the subscriber, who can then claim them against their own income for tax purposes — which is why flow-through financings are typically priced at a premium to a company's regular shares. Red Canyon's release did not state whether this placement carried that kind of premium.

The company said the proceeds qualify as flow-through critical mineral mining expenditures and are earmarked for exploration on its Kendal and Osiris projects and other eligible British Columbia properties. The expenditure deadline is December 31, 2027, and the related renunciation of the expenses to subscribers is effective December 31, 2026, the company said.

Red Canyon paid finder's fees of $24,025 in connection with the offering. Insiders of the company purchased 937,500 of the flow-through units, the release said, without further detail on which insiders participated or in what amounts individually.

All securities issued under the offering — the flow-through shares and the warrants — are restricted from trading until February 27, 2027. The offering closed October 26, 2026.

Red Canyon is a Vancouver-based company exploring in British Columbia, trading on the CSE under REDC, on the OTCQB under REDRF, and in Frankfurt under I91. The release did not disclose the current size of the company's exploration programs beyond naming the Kendal and Osiris projects as a focus for the new proceeds.
