Rush Gold adds two Nevada gold properties and a 40-year mining veteran as chairman

The CSE-listed junior also closed a second placement tranche for $1,030,500 as it builds out its Walker Lane Trend portfolio.

Capital Markets Bulletin · October 28, 2026 · Source: TheNewswire.ca · CSE:RGN

Rush Gold Corp. (CSE: RGN, FSE: B6H) said it has completed the acquisition of Landy Investments Ltd., through which the company gained the rights to two mineral properties in Nevada's Walker Lane Trend: the Hollow North-South project and the Douglas Canyon project. The release does not state the consideration paid for Landy Investments — no share issuance or cash amount is disclosed for the transaction.

Hollow North-South, a copper-gold property, is located about 15 kilometres east of Yerington, Nevada, on 109 claims on land managed by the U.S. Bureau of Land Management. Rush Gold holds an 80% interest in the project, which the company said complements its existing Skylight and Legal Tender projects.

Douglas Canyon, a gold-antimony property, is located about 80 kilometres northwest of Tonopah, Nevada, and covers 138 claims on BLM land. The release gives no interest percentage for Douglas Canyon, unlike the 80% stake disclosed for Hollow North-South, and does not state the terms on which Rush Gold acquired its rights to the property.

Rush Gold also appointed Andy Tudor as chairman. The company described Tudor as a geologist and mining executive with more than 40 years of experience across the mineral exploration and mining sectors. The release gives no further biographical detail.

Separately, the company closed the second tranche of a non-brokered private placement, issuing 10,305,000 shares for gross proceeds of $1,030,500. The release does not disclose a price per share, warrant terms, or a closing date for the tranche, and does not name an agent or finder.

Rush Gold said proceeds from the financing are intended to advance exploration on its existing and newly acquired mineral projects. The company reported cash on hand of $706,836 as of September 30, 2026, and said it had approximately 53.2 million shares outstanding.

The company also said it has engaged CPS Capital Group and EuroSwiss Capital Partners on advisory, communications and marketing work intended to strengthen market awareness. Rush Gold said planned exploration activity across its Nevada projects includes geological mapping, geochemical sampling, geophysics and drill testing, without giving a timeline for any of the programs.
