Elon Musk's Tesla and SpaceX have shed $456 billion since the midterms

Shares of both companies fell sharply this week as Democrats won control of Congress and unseated Musk allies in Texas, trimming his fortune by about $90 billion.

Capital Markets Bulletin · November 6, 2026 · Source: CNBC · NASDAQ:TSLA, NASDAQ:SPCX

Tesla shares fell about 11% and SpaceX shares fell about 9% on Thursday afternoon, CNBC reported, extending a slide that began after Tuesday's midterm election results were released. The declines pushed the two companies' combined market-capitalization losses past $420 billion since Tuesday's close, and by the end of Thursday's session CNBC said the combined loss had grown to about $456 billion.

CNBC attributed the selloff to investor reaction to the midterm election results, in which Democrats won control of both chambers of Congress. In Texas, where both Tesla and SpaceX are headquartered, Democratic candidates defeated longtime allies of Elon Musk, including gubernatorial candidate Gina Hinojosa, who defeated incumbent Governor Greg Abbott, according to the report.

The report cited several specific investor concerns: the possibility of congressional probes into Musk and his companies, stricter regulatory oversight of artificial-intelligence infrastructure and data centers, potential delays to SpaceX's compute-capacity buildout in Texas, scrutiny of SpaceX's Starlink restrictions in Ukraine, and regulatory obstacles to a potential merger between Tesla and SpaceX.

The decline also affected Musk's personal fortune, which CNBC said had topped $1 trillion following SpaceX's initial public offering in June and has since fallen below $800 billion, a drop of roughly $90 billion over the past week, though CNBC noted he remains the world's richest person. Musk had contributed more than $470 million to Republican candidates and causes during the midterm campaign, and CNBC reported that most of the candidates he backed lost their races.
