New Break Resources raises $523,820 to keep drilling near Timmins

The non-brokered flow-through financing funds continued drilling at the company's Moray gold project, about 49 kilometres southeast of Timmins, Ontario.

Capital Markets Bulletin · November 4, 2026 · Source: Investing News Network — Resource Investing · CSE:NBRK, OTCQB:NBRKF, FSE:O91

New Break Resources Ltd. (CSE: NBRK, OTCQB: NBRKF, FSE: O91) has closed a non-brokered private-placement flow-through financing, issuing 2,381,000 common shares that qualify as flow-through shares at a price of $0.22 per share, for gross proceeds of $523,820.

Flow-through shares let a mining explorer pass eligible Canadian Exploration Expenses to the purchaser rather than claim the deduction itself; New Break said it will renounce the expenses to purchasers of the FT Shares with an effective date no later than December 31, 2026.

The company paid $12,012 in finder's fees and issued 54,600 finder warrants, each exercisable at $0.22 for a period of 24 months.

Patricia Quigley, identified by the company as a control person, purchased 455,000 of the FT Shares in the financing.

The securities are subject to a statutory hold period of four months and one day, expiring March 4, 2027.

New Break said proceeds will be used for Canadian Exploration Expenses in connection with ongoing drilling at the company's Moray gold project, located approximately 49 kilometres southeast of Timmins, Ontario.
