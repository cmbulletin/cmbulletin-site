TotalEnergies takes a 30% stake in gas fields holding 6.5 trillion cubic feet

TotalEnergies will hold the 30% operated stake in Malaysia's Lang Lebah and B14 fields, pending regulatory approval.

Capital Markets Bulletin · November 2, 2026 · Source: Investing News Network — Resource Investing · NYSE:TTE, LSE:TTE, PARIS:TTE

TotalEnergies has signed agreements with PETRONAS Carigali Sdn Bhd, PTTEP and KUFPEC to acquire a 30% operated interest in the Lang Lebah and B14 gas fields off the coast of Sarawak, Malaysia, the company said November 1. Once the transactions close, TotalEnergies will hold the 30% operated stake alongside PTTEP and KUFPEC at 25% each and PETRONAS Carigali at 20%. Completion is subject to regulatory approvals; TotalEnergies gave no timeline for closing.

The company described Lang Lebah as one of Malaysia's largest offshore gas discoveries, with the nearby B14 field planned for development as part of the same integrated project. TotalEnergies said the combined fields could unlock more than 6.5 trillion cubic feet of gas resources, equivalent to more than 1 billion barrels of oil, feeding the existing Malaysia LNG liquefaction plant at a production plateau of around 10 billion cubic metres a year.

TotalEnergies chief executive Patrick Pouyanné said the agreements mark "another important step in our strategy to strengthen our integrated gas position in Malaysia." The three agreements are subject to the same condition — regulatory approvals in Malaysia — before completion, and the release gives no date for either the approvals or the closings, nor does it state how the fields were held before this agreement.
