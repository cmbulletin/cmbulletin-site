Aether Global pushes InMotion Energy deal deadline three weeks to Nov. 21

The defence-and-security technology company and clean-energy fleet firm InMotion now have until November 21 to sign a definitive agreement under their proposed business combination.

Capital Markets Bulletin · November 3, 2026 · Source: TheNewswire.ca · CSE:AETH, OTC:AETHF

Aether Global Innovations Corp. (CSE: AETH, OTC: AETHF), a defence and security technology company, has extended the exclusivity period and term of its binding letter of intent with InMotion Energy Corp. by three weeks, moving the deadline to November 21 from October 31. The company said all other terms of the original LOI, first signed September 28, remain unchanged.

The proposed transaction would combine Aether's unmanned-systems and counter-drone business with InMotion, which operates through its subsidiary Deecell Inc. developing solar power systems for transportation fleets. Aether described the pairing as a proposed business combination between the two companies, which the release classifies as a "Fundamental Change" under Canadian Securities Exchange policy.

Completing the deal requires the parties to execute a definitive agreement, obtain shareholder approval, and secure CSE approval, none of which have yet occurred. The companies said they continue to work toward finalizing those steps by the new November 21 deadline, and the release states there is no assurance the transaction will be completed.

The announcement does not disclose financial terms for the proposed combination, an exchange ratio, or a valuation for either company. Both Aether and InMotion remain separately listed companies while the parties negotiate toward a definitive agreement, and the release gives no specifics beyond the extended timeline and the classification of the deal as a Fundamental Change.

Today's release is itself short: it extends one deadline and confirms no other term has moved. Readers following either company should expect the next disclosure to be either a definitive agreement or confirmation that the proposed combination has lapsed at the new November 21 deadline.

To verify independently

  • The release does not disclose financial terms, an exchange ratio, or a valuation for the proposed business combination.
  • There is no assurance stated in the release that the transaction will be completed by the new deadline or at all.
