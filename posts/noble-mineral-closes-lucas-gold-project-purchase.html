Noble Mineral closes Lucas Gold Project purchase

The TSX Venture-listed explorer closed its acquisition of the project from Canada Nickel Company and separately received exchange final acceptance for the transactions.

October 21, 2026TheNewswire.caTSXV:NOB, OTCQB:NLPXF

Noble Mineral Exploration Inc. (TSX-V: NOB) (FRANKFURT: NB7) (OTCQB: NLPXF) announced October 21, 2026 that it has closed the transactions under a Purchase Agreement with Canada Nickel Company Inc., under which Noble acquires the Lucas Gold Project. The transactions were previously announced in Noble's news releases dated July 13, 2026 and September 17, 2026.

The company said Noble has received final acceptance from the TSX Venture Exchange for the transactions under the agreement. No further conditions or approvals are described in this announcement.

For the main terms and conditions of the Purchase Agreement and a description of the Lucas Gold Project, the release directs readers to the news release Noble issued on September 17, 2026, rather than restating them here.

This announcement discloses no purchase price, share or cash consideration, royalty, or other transaction term, and gives no description of the project's location, size, geology or exploration history. It states no use of proceeds and no next procedural step beyond the exchange acceptance already obtained.

Noble describes itself, in the release's standard company description, as a Canadian-based junior exploration company that holds securities in Canada Nickel Company Inc., among other listed holdings. The release lists H. Vance White, President, as the contact for further information.

Taken together, the two releases Noble points to — October 21 confirming closing and exchange acceptance, and September 17 setting out the terms — mean a reader following only today's announcement sees confirmation that a previously announced acquisition has completed, without the underlying deal terms restated in the same document.

To verify independently

  • The purchase agreement's terms, including any consideration paid for the Lucas Gold Project, are not disclosed in this release and are described only in Noble's September 17, 2026 release.
