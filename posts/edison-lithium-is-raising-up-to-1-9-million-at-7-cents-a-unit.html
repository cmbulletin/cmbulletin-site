Edison Lithium is raising up to $1.9 million at 7 cents a unit

The non-brokered placement pairs each share with a warrant exercisable at 10 cents for three years, funding lithium exploration work.

Capital Markets Bulletin · October 28, 2026 · Source: Investing News Network — Resource Investing · TSXV:EDDY

Edison Lithium Corp. (TSXV: EDDY, FSE: VV0) said it intends to complete a non-brokered private placement of up to 27,142,857 units at $0.07 per unit, for gross proceeds of up to approximately $1,900,000. Edison is a junior exploration company focused on the procurement, exploration and development of cobalt, lithium, alkali and other energy-metal properties.

Each unit consists of one common share of Edison plus one common-share purchase warrant. Each warrant entitles the holder to buy one additional share at $0.10 for 36 months from the date of issuance. In a non-brokered placement, the company sells the units directly to investors it has found itself, rather than through an underwriter who commits to sell the full offering.

The securities issued under the offering will be subject to a statutory hold period of four months and one day from the date of issuance, the release said — the standard resale restriction under Canadian securities law for securities distributed without a prospectus.

Insiders of the company may participate in the offering, which the release says would constitute a related-party transaction; Edison said it anticipates relying on exemptions from the formal valuation and minority-shareholder-approval requirements under Multilateral Instrument 61-101, subject to satisfying the applicable conditions. The release does not name which insiders, if any, intend to participate.

The company may also pay finder's fees in connection with the offering, subject to compliance with the policies of the TSX Venture Exchange and applicable securities law, Edison said. The release does not name a finder or state a fee amount, and no agent or underwriter is identified anywhere in the announcement.

Edison said the net proceeds will be used for exploration activities and for general working capital and corporate purposes. Completion remains subject to final acceptance by the TSX Venture Exchange. The release does not state an expected closing date, and the units have not been registered under the U.S. Securities Act, so they may not be offered or sold within the United States.
