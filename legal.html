Where no reliable free source exists for a figure, we omit it rather than estimate it — an absent row is deliberate.

Forward-looking information

Reports frequently describe an issuer's stated plans, intentions, timelines or expectations. That is forward-looking information: it reflects what the issuer said at the time, not what will happen. Exploration results, financings that have been announced but not closed, and conditions still outstanding may never come to pass. We do not undertake to update an entry as circumstances change, beyond correcting errors of fact.

How entries are produced

Entries are drafted with the assistance of a large language model working from the primary sources listed on each entry, and are then checked by a separate automated editorial review that reads the draft back against those sources and holds anything it cannot verify. Held items are not published until a person resolves them. This process is described plainly because readers are entitled to know how what they are reading was made; it reduces the risk of error but does not eliminate it.
