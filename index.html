Salesforce agrees to buy voice-AI startup Cresta in a deal reported at $2.7 billion

The company has not disclosed terms; Cresta's voice agents already serve Marriott, Comcast and Alaska Airlines, and the deal should close early in fiscal 2028.

Salesforce has signed a definitive agreement to acquire Cresta, an artificial intelligence company whose voice agents handle customer service calls and coach human representatives in real time. Salesforce's own announcement names no purchase price; The Information has reported the deal at approximately $2.7 billion. Cresta already serves Marriott, Comcast and Alaska Airlines. Read the full story

The acquisition pairs Cresta's voice technology with Salesforce's Service Cloud and Agentforce products, extending the company's customer-service push from chat into phone calls. Kishan Chetan, who leads Agentforce and Service Cloud, said voice is 'where service is hardest to get right.' The deal is expected to close in the first quarter of Salesforce's 2028 fiscal year, subject to regulatory approval. Read the full story
