DoorDash offers $65 a share, valuing Instacart at about $17 billion

The cash-and-stock bid is a 26% premium to Instacart's October 22 closing price, with the deal targeted to close by the end of 2027 or the first half of 2028.

DoorDash has agreed to acquire Instacart, operated by Maplebear Inc., in a deal valuing the grocery-delivery company at about $17 billion. Instacart shareholders can choose $65.00 in cash per share, 0.3372 of a DoorDash share, or a mix of $48.75 cash plus 0.0843 of a DoorDash share for each share they hold. Read the full story

Instacart's board approved the deal unanimously, and holders of about 19% of its shares have agreed to vote for it. DoorDash will fund the cash portion from cash on hand and debt, including a 364-day bridge loan from JPMorgan Chase, with closing targeted for the end of 2027 or the first half of 2028, subject to shareholder and antitrust approval.
