Steadright's insiders are expected to buy most of the second tranche

Insiders already took 1,100,000 units of Steadright's first tranche at $0.10, and the company expects them to take most of the rest.

Steadright Critical Minerals Inc. (CSE: SCM), which explores for critical minerals in Morocco, has closed the first tranche of a non-brokered private placement, raising $530,000 at $0.10 per unit. Company insiders subscribed for 1,100,000 of the units in this tranche, at the same price. Read the full story

Each unit is one common share and one warrant, exercisable at $0.16 per share for 24 months. The securities carry a four-month-and-one-day hold period. The private placement has been upsized by $100,000 to a total of $900,000, with the second and final tranche expected to close within the week. Read the full story
