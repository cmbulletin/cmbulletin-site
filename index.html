Aeon Strategies' crypto audit delay pushes its filing to December 31

The CSE-listed company has applied for a management cease-trade order to avoid a broader halt while its auditor works through complex crypto-related holdings.

Aeon Strategies Ltd. (CSE: AEON), formerly Cloud3 Ventures Inc., said its auditor now expects to complete its annual financial statements by December 31, 2026 — later than the December 4 deadline the company had previously flagged. Davidson & Company LLP cited limited availability and the added time needed for the company's crypto-related investments. Read the full story

To avoid a general trading halt, Aeon has applied for a management cease trade order, which would bar its CEO and CFO from trading the company's shares until the filings are complete — rather than freezing trading for all shareholders. If regulators do not grant it, a general cease trade order covering all trading remains possible. Read the full story
