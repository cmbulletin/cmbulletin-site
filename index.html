Bastion Square Partners II raises $1.5 million in its TSX Venture debut

The Vancouver capital pool company sold 15 million shares at 10 cents each and will look for an acquisition target to complete its qualifying transaction.

Bastion Square Partners II Inc., a Vancouver-based capital pool company, closed its initial public offering on November 3, 2026, raising $1.5 million in gross proceeds through the sale of 15,000,000 common shares at $0.10 each. The shares listed on the TSX Venture Exchange that day under a trading halt pending closing. Read the full story

Trading in the shares, under the symbol BASQ.P, is set to resume November 9, 2026. The company now has 27,000,000 shares outstanding, of which 13,825,000 are held in escrow. Independent Trading Group (ITG), Inc. acted as agent, earning a $55,000 cash commission, a $10,000 corporate finance fee and 500,000 warrants exercisable at $0.10 until November 3, 2028.
