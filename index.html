Ukrainian drones set fire to a major Ozon warehouse in Russia

The attack hit Adygeysk for a second time, even as Ozon had already projected $367 million in losses from strikes on 11 of its sites.

A Ukrainian drone strike set fire to a logistics hub operated by Russian e-commerce retailer Ozon in Adygeysk, in southern Russia, on November 4, 2026, the company and a regional official said. Debris also damaged two nearby villages. Ozon said it was not aware of any casualties and that all staff had been evacuated. Read the full story

It was the second strike on this facility. Ukraine has targeted dozens of warehouses run by Ozon and rival Wildberries since July as part of a campaign against Russia's economy and war effort. Ozon said it would cancel orders it cannot fulfil and temporarily stop accepting deliveries from sellers at the site; its shares fell 0.4%. Read the full story
