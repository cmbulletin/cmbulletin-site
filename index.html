Salesforce agrees to buy voice-AI startup Cresta in a deal reported at $2.7 billion

The company has not disclosed terms; Cresta's voice agents already serve Marriott, Comcast and Alaska Airlines, and the deal should close early in fiscal 2028.

Salesforce has signed a definitive agreement to acquire Cresta, an artificial intelligence company whose voice agents handle customer service calls and coach human representatives in real time. Salesforce's own announcement names no purchase price; The Information has reported the deal at approximately $2.7 billion. Cresta already serves Marriott, Comcast and Alaska Airlines. The acquisition pairs Cresta's voice technology with Salesforce's Service Cloud and Agentforce products, extending the company's customer-service push from chat into phone calls. Kishan Chetan, who leads Agentforce and Service Cloud, said voice is 'where service is hardest to get right.' News

A SpaceX-Tesla merger could hand Musk 56% voting control

Barclays estimates the combination would give Musk roughly 37% ownership of the combined company, without triggering the $1-trillion Tesla pay package shareholders approved.

A SpaceX-Tesla merger would give Elon Musk about 56% of the combined company's voting power, Barclays analysts estimate, versus the roughly 29% he would hold even if he meets every milestone under the $1-trillion Tesla pay package shareholders already approved, Reuters reported.

SpaceX's own compensation plan hands Musk 85% voting control immediately, without operational milestones, with the stock's value tied to targets including Mars colonization and data-center buildouts worth up to $719 billion at the highest level. Tesla's $2-trillion market-cap threshold for any merger-linked award compares with its current $1.6-trillion valuation.
