Organigram offers $561 million to beat Curaleaf's hostile bid for Aurora Cannabis

The $7.80-a-share offer, payable in cash, stock or a mix of both, tops Curaleaf Holdings' hostile US$5 bid by about 21 per cent.

Organigram Global Inc. has agreed to acquire Aurora Cannabis Inc. in a transaction the companies valued at approximately $561 million, offering $7.80 per share. Aurora shareholders may elect cash only, 5.86 Organigram shares, or a combination of $3.75 in cash and 3.04 Organigram shares, the companies said. Read the full story

The offer is a roughly 21-per-cent premium to Curaleaf Holdings' hostile US$5-a-share bid, which Aurora had been fending off for months. Organigram is funding the deal with a $188-million private placement from British American Tobacco and $210 million in ATB Financial loan facilities. A shareholder vote is expected in December.
