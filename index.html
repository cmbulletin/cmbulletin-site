Binance says customers pulled $4.7 billion after its $6.3 billion hack

The exchange reopened withdrawals after a 48-hour freeze following the theft, which the FBI has linked to the North Korean hacking group TraderTraitor.

Binance said Monday that customers had withdrawn a net $4.72 billion since the exchange reopened for withdrawals on Sunday morning, after freezing them for roughly 48 hours following a $6.3 billion theft the company has called potentially the largest financial heist in history. The FBI has attributed the hack to the North Korean group TraderTraitor. Read the full story

Binance co-chief executive Yi He said the exchange's "top priority right now is to ensure the environment is secure, after which users can withdraw their funds." The company said it is covering customer losses with company funds, having already raised $6 billion from its corporate treasury, an insurance fund and reserves set aside for security breaches.
