OpenAI cuts its funding target by $10 billion as AI stocks slide

The ChatGPT maker now seeks $20 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation, down from a $30 billion target.

OpenAI has cut the target for its latest funding round by $10 billion, now seeking $20 billion rather than the $30 billion it had been pursuing, according to a Financial Times report carried by Reuters. The company is also seeking a lower valuation of $1.25 trillion, down from the $1.4 trillion it wanted. Read the full story

The cut comes as a tech-sector selloff tied to worries about AI spending has hit markets; the Nasdaq fell 2.9% the day the news broke and is down 7.8% from its October 6 peak. Some investors have reduced commitments, and the round's 'first close' is expected to land below the new $20 billion target. Read the full story
