SK Hynix is handing shareholders $75 billion after a sevenfold profit jump

The chipmaker's operating profit rose more than sevenfold to 83.3 trillion won as AI-driven demand for memory chips sold out capacity into 2029.

SK Hynix said it will return approximately 100 trillion won ($75 billion) to shareholders, after third-quarter operating profit surged more than sevenfold to 83.3 trillion won on revenue of 104.6 trillion won, up 328% from a year earlier. The return comprises roughly 30 trillion won of dividends and 60 trillion won of buybacks and cancellations.

Shares rose as much as 9.4%. Chief financial officer Kim Woo-hyun said AI investment momentum is translating into broader adoption of AI services and tangible business results. The company has sold out its HBM and DRAM chip production through 2029 and has signed long-term agreements with 14 major customers. Read the full story
