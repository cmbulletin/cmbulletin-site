Energy

Saudi Aramco's profit jumps 34% to $36.25 billion despite attacks on its facilities

Elevated crude and product prices drove the profit rise, and the board authorized a $21.9 billion dividend, even as Houthi strikes hit its facilities and Iranian activity disrupted Hormuz shipping.

Saudi Arabian Oil Co. reported third-quarter net profit of $36.25 billion, up 34% from a year earlier, as revenue climbed to $140.5 billion from $111.6 billion. The company attributed the increase to higher prices for crude oil and refined chemical products, even as sales volumes fell.

The results followed Houthi missile and drone attacks on Aramco facilities, including its Dhahran headquarters and refining sites at Jazan and Riyadh, plus Iranian activity disrupting Strait of Hormuz shipping. Chief executive Amin Nasser said the attacks "continued to amplify the global energy supply crisis" in the quarter. The board authorized a $21.9 billion dividend, up 3.5% year on year.
